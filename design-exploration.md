# Design Exploration

This archive preserves the seven design candidates and the homepage exploration that led to the approved [Contemporary Cinema Design System v1.0](design.md). The status labels below reflect the candidate notes from the exploration phase.

## Selected Direction

- Contemporary Cinema uses the light Cinematic Editorial palette and adapts the visual treatment to each chapter.
- Prototype C, *A Life in Chapters*, combines the expressive typography of Title Sequence with the expansive photography of Cinematic Opening. Its two primary chapter entrances are Personal Life and Professional Experience.
- Cinematic Rhythm is the selected motion direction. The approved specification records its interaction patterns and reduced-motion requirements.

## Design Candidate 01 — Cinematic Editorial

**Status:** Exploring

**Primary Reference:** *Hacks* (HBO) opening title cards.

**Design Direction:** Mid-century cinematic aesthetics, expressive typography, editorial composition, analog photography, and intentional color palettes.

**Core Concept:** A personal website experienced as a collection of cinematic chapters rather than a conventional portfolio.

**Design Principles**

- Typography as part of the visual storytelling.
- Photography with a strong sense of atmosphere.
- Distinct visual identities for different chapters.
- Expressive yet restrained design.
- Professional clarity without sacrificing personality.

## Design Candidate 02 — Independent Magazine

**Status:** Exploring

**Primary References:** Kinfolk and Cereal magazines.

**Design Direction:** Editorial layouts, refined typography, generous whitespace, atmospheric photography, and thoughtful visual hierarchy.

**Core Concept:** A personal website experienced as an evolving independent magazine, with distinct sections reflecting personal interests, life experiences, and professional insights.

**Design Principles**

- Strong editorial composition.
- Photography as a storytelling medium.
- Thoughtful use of whitespace and typography.
- A balance between visual sophistication and readability.
- A structure that accommodates ongoing content creation.

## Design Candidate 03 — Personal Archive

**Status:** Exploring

**Primary References:** Personal archives, travel journals, photographic contact sheets, and collected ephemera.

**Design Direction:** Curated collections, archival layouts, analog textures, chronological organization, and personal artifacts.

**Core Concept:** A living archive of experiences, interests, memories, and personal growth that visitors can freely explore.

## Design Candidate 04 — Cinematic Minimalism

**Status:** Exploring

**Primary Reference:** *Interstellar* (Christopher Nolan).

**Design Direction:** Expansive compositions, atmospheric photography, restrained color palettes, natural lighting, and subtle motion.

**Core Concept:** A personal website that conveys the passage of time, the vastness of the world, and the intimacy of individual experiences through cinematic simplicity.

**Design Principles**

- Generous negative space and immersive photography.
- Emotional storytelling through visual restraint.
- Strong contrast between human intimacy and expansive environments.
- Slow, purposeful transitions that create a sense of time.
- Minimal typography with clear visual hierarchy.
- Muted tones inspired by natural landscapes and cinematic lighting.

**Suggested Applications:** Homepage, personal introduction, professional experience, and life timeline.

## Design Candidate 07 — Cinematic Anthology

**Status:** Exploring

**Primary References:**

- *Hacks* — Cinematic editorial typography and chapter-specific visual identities.
- *Interstellar* — Spatial depth, emotional intimacy, and cinematic minimalism.
- *The Odyssey* — Exploration, naturalism, and epic storytelling.
- *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* — Urban energy and human-centered realism.

**Design Direction:** A cohesive cinematic design system that allows individual sections to express distinct moods, visual identities, and narrative rhythms.

**Core Concept:** A personal website experienced as an anthology of cinematic chapters, each revealing a different dimension of an evolving life rather than presenting a conventional portfolio.

**Design Principles**

- Organize content into meaningful narrative chapters.
- Allow each chapter to develop its own visual identity.
- Maintain consistency through shared navigation, typography foundations, layout grids, and interaction patterns.
- Use photography, typography, color, and motion as storytelling tools.
- Create intentional emotional transitions between different sections.
- Balance immersive personal expression with professional clarity.
- Prioritize authenticity over decorative cinematic effects.
- Preserve accessibility, usability, and performance across all chapters.
